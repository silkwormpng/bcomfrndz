CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION & THE PAYROLL LIFECYCLE

Payroll is much more than simply distributing money on the last day of the month. It is a critical, highly sensitive business process governed by strict labor laws, tax regulations, and accounting standards. A single error can lead to disgruntled employees, massive statutory penalties, and legal disputes.

The Three Phases of Payroll:
- Pre-Payroll: Gathering data (attendance, new joins, exits, salary revisions, tax declarations).
- Payroll Run: Actual calculation of gross earnings, deductions, and net pay.
- Post-Payroll: Disbursing salaries, paying statutory dues (PF, ESI, TDS), recording expenses.
Golden Rule: 100% Accuracy, Zero Delays, Strict Confidentiality.

CHAPTER 2: DEMYSTIFYING CTC, GROSS, AND NET PAY

2.1 Cost to Company (CTC)

CTC = Gross Salary + Employer Statutory Contributions + Benefits (insurance, subsidized meals, gratuity).

2.2 Gross Salary

Basic Salary: 40-50% of CTC – base for PF & gratuity.
HRA: Usually 40% or 50% of Basic.
Special Allowance: Fully taxable balancing figure.
Conveyance / LTA / Medical: Specific tax-governed allowances.

2.3 Net Salary (Take-Home)

Net Salary = Gross Salary – (Employee Statutory Deductions + Income Tax/TDS + Voluntary Deductions).

CHAPTER 3: ESSENTIAL PRE-PAYROLL INPUTS

  • Attendance & Leave Data (Loss of Pay days)
  • New Joiner Data (DoJ, CTC, PAN, UAN, bank details) – pro-rata salary
  • Exit Data – moved to Full & Final settlement
  • Salary Revisions / Appraisals effective in month
  • Investment Declarations (Form 12BB, rent receipts, 80C proofs for TDS)

CHAPTER 4: DECODING STATUTORY COMPLIANCES

4.1 Provident Fund (EPF)

Applicable: 20+ employees. Base = Basic+DA (ceiling ₹15,000/month). Employee: 12% | Employer: 12% (split: 3.67% EPF + 8.33% EPS).

4.2 Employee State Insurance (ESI)

Applicable: 10+ employees, gross salary ≤ ₹21,000. Employee: 0.75% | Employer: 3.25% of gross.

4.3 Professional Tax (PT)

State-level slab. Example: Maharashtra ₹200/month (₹300 in Feb); Karnataka flat ₹200 above threshold.

4.4 TDS / Income Tax

Based on employee’s projected annual income, chosen regime, investments. Monthly TDS = (Annual tax liability / 12).

CHAPTER 10: FULL & FINAL (F&F) SETTLEMENTS AND YEAR-END

F&F Settlement (separate from monthly payroll):
- Leave Encashment (unavailed earned leaves)
- Gratuity (if completed 5+ years)
- Recover notice period shortfall & unreturned assets.
Year-End Activities (March/April):
- Form 16 (Part A & B) generation for all employees by May.
- Form 24Q (Quarterly TDS return) – Q4 includes annual salary annexure.

CHAPTER 11: WORKSHOP FAQs & COMMON PITFALLS

Q1: Employee joined on 10th of a 30-day month. Salary calculation?
A: Pro-rata: active days = 21 (30-9). Basic = (Monthly Basic/30)*21. All allowances pro-rated accordingly.
Q2: Missed TDS deduction for 3 months – can we deduct all at once?
A: Yes, adjust in remaining months, but it will drastically reduce take-home that month. Always compute projected annual tax from start of FY.
Q3: Is bonus mandatory under Payment of Bonus Act?
A: If 20+ employees and employee basic ≤ ₹21,000, statutory bonus min 8.33% (max 20%) is mandatory for companies with 5 years or profits.
Q4: Absconding employee – process payroll?
A: Mark 100% LOP, put payout "On Hold". Process via F&F after legal formalities to recover dues.
Q5: Employee’s salary crosses ₹21,000 mid-year – ESI stops immediately?
A: No! ESI works in Contribution Periods (Apr-Sept, Oct-Mar). If crossing in June, ESI continues until Sept, stops from Oct. Critical trap!
⚠️ Critical Reminder: Statutory deadlines (PF/ESI by 15th, TDS by 7th) are strict. Delays attract heavy penalties.
📘 Workshop Note: Payroll requires rigorous documentation and audit trail. Always retain attendance registers, investment proofs, and statutory challans for at least 7 years. Use standard HRMS for accuracy.
