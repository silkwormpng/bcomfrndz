CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION & THE LEGAL MANDATE

Filing an Income Tax Return (ITR) is not merely about claiming a refund; it is a mandatory legal declaration of your annual earnings to the Government of India under Section 139(1) of the Income Tax Act, 1961. For salaried individuals, the employer is legally obligated to deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) based on the employee's declared investments and deposit it with the government. However, the employer's calculation is only an estimate based on what you told them. Filing the ITR is your final, personal reconciliation with the government.

Consequences of Non-Filing or Late Filing: The standard deadline for salaried individuals whose accounts do not require an audit is July 31st of the Assessment Year.
- Late Fees (Section 234F): ₹5,000 (₹1,000 if total income below ₹5 Lakhs).
- Penal Interest (Section 234A): 1% interest per month on outstanding tax.
- Loss of Benefits: Cannot carry forward certain losses, higher future TDS rates.

CHAPTER 2: DEMYSTIFYING TAX REGIMES & SLABS (AY 2024-25)

Financial Year 2023-24 (Income: 1 Apr 2023 – 31 Mar 2024) → Assessment Year 2024-25

2.1 The Default: New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC)

Starting AY 2024-25, the New Tax Regime is the default. Pros: lower slabs, simpler. Cons: most deductions forfeited (80C, 80D, HRA, LTA, home loan interest for self-occupied property). Allowed: Standard Deduction ₹50,000, employer NPS [80CCD(2)]. Rebate u/s 87A: income up to ₹7,00,000 = zero tax.

2.2 The Alternative: Old Tax Regime

You must consciously “opt-out” of the New Regime on the portal. Highly beneficial if HRA, home loans, max 80C/80D investments. Rebate u/s 87A: income up to ₹5,00,000 = zero tax.

CHAPTER 3: ESSENTIAL PRE-FILING DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

  • PAN Card & Aadhaar Card (ensure linked – unlinked PAN becomes inoperative)
  • Form 16 (Part A & B) from employer(s)
  • Bank Statements (Apr 1 – Mar 31 of FY)
  • Form 16A/16B/16C (TDS on interest, rent, etc.)
  • Investment proofs: Rent receipts & landlord’s PAN (if rent > ₹1L/year), Home loan interest certificate, Life/Health insurance premium receipts, Donation receipts (with donee’s PAN for 80G)

CHAPTER 4: DECODING FORM 16, 26AS, AIS, AND TIS

Form 16: Part A – TAN, PAN, month-wise TDS summary. Part B – salary breakdown, exemptions, net taxable salary.
Form 26AS: Official tax ledger showing all TDS/TCS against your PAN. Golden Rule: If TDS is in Form 16 but not in 26AS, government will NOT give credit.
AIS (Annual Information Statement): Detailed transaction-level surveillance (mutual funds, dividends, interest).
TIS (Taxpayer Information Summary): Summarized version; portal uses TIS to pre-fill ITR.

CHAPTER 6: SELECTING THE RIGHT ITR FORM

ITR-1 (Sahaj): For resident individuals, total income up to ₹50 Lakhs, income from Salary, ONE house property, and Other Sources (interest, dividends, agri up to ₹5k).
ITR-2: Income > ₹50 Lakhs, capital gains, multiple house properties, directorship/unlisted shares.
ITR-3/ITR-4: Business / presumptive taxation. This workshop focuses strictly on ITR-1.

CHAPTER 7: STEP-BY-STEP ITR-1 FILING: THE 5 PILLARS

Initiating: e-File → Income Tax Returns → File Income Tax Return → Select AY 2024-25, Mode: Online, Individual, ITR-1 → "Let's Get Started".

📸 [Screenshot: Start_New_Filing_ITR1.png] – Select AY and ITR-1 option.

🏛️ PILLAR 1: Personal Information & Bank Linking

Verify demographics, name matches PAN. Nature of Employment: For private sector select "Others". Filing section: 139(1) (on time) / 139(4) (belated) / 139(5) (revised).
Regime Selection (Crucial): "Do you wish to exercise option u/s 115BAC(6) of opting out of New Tax Regime?" → YES = Old Regime, NO = New Regime (default).

📸 [Screenshot: Personal_Info_Nature_of_Employment.png] – dropdown & regime radio buttons.

Bank Details: Select pre-validated account for refund credit → Confirm.

📊 PILLAR 2: Gross Total Income

Part A: Salary – Compare pre-filled numbers with Form 16 Part B. Exempt allowances: HRA (10(13A)), LTA (10(5)), Standard Deduction ₹50,000 auto-applied, Professional Tax u/s 16(iii).

📸 [Screenshot: Editing_Salary_Exemptions.png] – Exempt allowances dropdown showing HRA.

Part B: House Property
- Self-occupied: enter home loan interest (max ₹2,00,000 under Old Regime).
- Let-out: gross rent received, 30% deduction auto, municipal taxes, full interest (loss set-off capped ₹2,00,000).
Part C: Other Sources – Pre-filled via TIS. Declare savings/FD interest, dividends, family pension. Verify bank statements.

💰 PILLAR 3: Total Deductions (Chapter VI-A)

⚠️ Important: If you selected New Regime (opted NOT to opt out), most deductions are not allowed. For Old Regime, manually claim:
Section 80C (max ₹1,50,000): EPF, PPF, ELSS, LIC, tuition fees, home loan principal.
80D (Health Insurance): Self/family up to ₹25,000; Parents (senior) up to ₹50,000. Preventive health checkup ₹5,000 (within limit).
80E: Education loan interest (no upper limit).
80TTA: Savings account interest up to ₹10,000.
80TTB: Senior citizens: up to ₹50,000 on all interest.
80G: Donations (e.g., PM Cares 100%, NGOs 50%) – require donee PAN.

📸 [Screenshot: Chapter_VIA_Deductions_Menu.png] – Deductions checklist for manual entry.

🧾 PILLAR 4: Tax Paid & Reconciliation

Read-only summary: TDS on Salary (match Form 16 Part A), TDS on other income (Form 16A), TCS, Advance Tax / Self-Assessment Tax payments. Troubleshooting: If employer TDS missing in this schedule, DO NOT proceed – your employer hasn’t filed TDS return, will cause demand notice.

📸 [Screenshot: Tax_Paid_TDS_Summary.png] – TDS from salary and other sources with deductor TAN details.

🧮 PILLAR 5: Total Tax Liability & Calculations

Automated calculator: Gross Total Income – Deductions = Taxable Income. Tax computed as per chosen regime slab. Rebate u/s 87A (if income ≤ ₹5L Old / ≤ ₹7L New) reduces tax to zero. 4% Cess added. Net Tax Liability = Gross Tax – Tax Paid (TDS). Confirm.

CHAPTER 8: VALIDATION, TAX PAYMENT & SUBMISSION

CHAPTER 9: THE CRUCIAL FINAL STEP: E-VERIFICATION

⚠️ LEGAL WARNING: An ITR is invalid and treated as "Not Filed" until verified within 30 days of submission.

Select "e-Verify Now" (recommended). Methods: Aadhaar OTP (easiest, must link mobile), Net Banking, Bank Account EVC, Demat EVC. Choose Aadhaar OTP → consent box → Generate Aadhaar OTP → enter 6-digit OTP → Validate.

CHAPTER 10: POST-FILING: INTIMATIONS, REVISIONS, AND REFUNDS

Processing by CPC Bengaluru: Cross-checks ITR with 26AS & AIS.
Intimation u/s 143(1): Password-protected PDF (PAN+DOB in lowercase).
Defective Return u/s 139(9): 15 days to correct.
Tracking Refunds: Login → e-File → View Filed Returns. Status: Submitted → Verified → Processing → Processed with Refund Due → Refund Issued (NEFT to pre-validated bank).
Revised Return u/s 139(5): Start new filing → in Personal Info select 139(5) – Revised Return → enter original Acknowledgement number & date → fix errors → submit & e-verify again. Revised return replaces original.

CHAPTER 11: WORKSHOP FAQs & COMMON PITFALLS

Q1: I changed jobs mid-year, two Form 16s. What do I do?
A: Aggregate Gross Salary from both. Both employers applied basic exemption separately – you will likely owe Self-Assessment Tax. Calculate and pay before filing.
Q2: HR rejected rent receipts. Can I still claim HRA?
A: Yes. In Old Regime, under Pillar 2 salary exemptions, manually enter HRA exemption (calculated: least of HRA received, 50% of basic (metro)/40% non-metro, rent paid minus 10% basic). Keep valid rent receipts & landlord PAN.
Q3: I traded shares (profit ₹5,000). Can I file ITR-1?
A: No. Capital gains require ITR-2. Filing ITR-1 will lead to defective return notice.
Q4: Do I need to declare EPF interest?
A: Interest on EPF up to contributions of ₹2.5L/year is exempt. However, exempt income should be reported in "Exempt Income" schedule.
Q5: Aadhaar mobile number not linked for OTP?
A: Either update mobile at Aadhaar Seva Kendra or use Net Banking e-verification (most major banks have “Income Tax e-Filing” link under Tax services) – verifies return without OTP.
