CHAPTER 1: FORWARD CHARGE VS. REVERSE CHARGE

Under normal GST regime, supplier collects tax from buyer and deposits it – Forward Charge Mechanism (FCM). To widen tax net and monitor unorganized sectors, Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) shifts the liability to the recipient.

Why RCM exists:
- Unorganized suppliers (farmers, freelance truck drivers) can't be expected to register.
- Import of services: Indian buyer must pay IGST under RCM because foreign supplier is outside Indian jurisdiction.

CHAPTER 2: LEGAL FRAMEWORK (CGST ACT, 2017)

Section 9 of CGST Act (and corresponding Section 5 of IGST Act) governs RCM.

Section 9(3): Notified goods & services – government specifies list where RCM is mandatory regardless of supplier's registration status.

Section 9(4): Purchases from Unregistered Dealers (URD) – originally any purchase from URD attracted RCM, but suspended. Currently applies mainly to real estate sector (promoters buying from unregistered dealers, shortfall attracts RCM at 18% or 28% for cement).

CHAPTER 3: RCM ON GOODS – KEY NOTIFIED CATEGORIES

If your business purchases these from agriculturists / specified suppliers, you (registered buyer) must pay GST under RCM:

  • Cashew nuts (not shelled or peeled) – from agriculturist
  • Bidi wrapper leaves (Tendu) – from agriculturist
  • Tobacco leaves – from agriculturist
  • Raw cotton – from agriculturist
  • Lottery – RCM paid by lottery distributor
  • Used vehicles, seized/confiscated goods, waste & scrap – when supplied by Central/State Government to registered person

CHAPTER 4: RCM ON SERVICES – HIGH-RISK BUSINESS SCENARIOS

4.1 Goods Transport Agency (GTA)

If GTA does not opt for forward charge (12% with ITC or 5% without ITC), the registered recipient must pay RCM @ 5% on freight. Always check GTA invoice declaration.

4.2 Legal Services (Advocates & Arbitral Tribunals)

Individual advocate or firm providing legal services to a business entity → 18% GST under RCM by business entity.

4.3 Remuneration to Directors

Salary (employer-employee) – no GST. Sitting fees / Commission (Non-Executive Directors) → 18% RCM by company.

4.4 Sponsorship Services

Body corporate paying sponsorship fees for any event → 18% RCM.

4.5 Security Services & Rent-a-Cab

Security personnel (supplied by any person other than body corporate) to registered person → RCM.
Renting of motor vehicle by non-body corporate (e.g., individual cab owner) to body corporate → RCM @ 5%.

4.6 Import of Services

Any service imported from outside India for business purposes → IGST under RCM (usually 18%).

CHAPTER 5: RCM & INPUT TAX CREDIT – THE "CASH FIRST" RULE

Golden Rule of RCM: You CANNOT use existing ITC balance to pay RCM liability. RCM must be paid in CASH (Electronic Cash Ledger).
Silver Lining: After paying RCM in cash, you can claim the exact same amount as Input Tax Credit in the same month's GSTR-3B (provided business use). Net tax cost = zero, but temporary working capital outflow.

CHAPTER 6: INVOICING RULES & DOCUMENTATION UNDER RCM

Self-Invoice (Section 31(3)(f)): If you purchase goods/services from an Unregistered Supplier that attract RCM, issue a Self-Invoice on date of receipt of goods/services. Header must clearly mention "TAX INVOICE ISSUED UNDER REVERSE CHARGE".

📸 [Screenshot: Self_Invoice_Format.png] – Standard invoice template with "REVERSE CHARGE" header and tax amounts.

Payment Voucher (Section 31(3)(g)): At the time of making payment to the supplier for an RCM supply, the recipient must issue a Payment Voucher (Supplier name, recipient GSTIN, amount paid, RCM tax amount).

CHAPTER 7: SYSTEM SETUP: ACCOUNTING ENTRIES FOR RCM (Tally/ERP)

Example: GTA freight bill of ₹10,000 (RCM @5% – CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%)

Step 1: Purchase/Expense Entry (create liability)

Debit: Freight/Transport Expense A/c          ₹10,000
Debit: RCM CGST Input A/c                     ₹250
Debit: RCM SGST Input A/c                     ₹250
Credit: Vendor (GTA) A/c                      ₹10,000
Credit: RCM CGST Payable A/c                  ₹250
Credit: RCM SGST Payable A/c                  ₹250
📸 [Screenshot: Tally_RCM_Purchase_Voucher.png] – Tally voucher with "Purchase From URD - Reverse Charge" classification.

Step 2: Payment to Government Entry (clear liability via cash)

Debit: RCM CGST Payable A/c                  ₹250
Debit: RCM SGST Payable A/c                  ₹250
Credit: Bank A/c                              ₹500
📸 [Screenshot: Tally_RCM_Tax_Payment_JV.png] – Journal/Payment voucher clearing RCM payable ledgers via bank.

Note: RCM Input A/c (created in Step 1) becomes ITC asset, available to offset normal outward liability.

GSTR-9 Reporting: Table 4G – total RCM liability paid; Table 6C & 6D – RCM ITC claimed (segregated into purchases from Registered and Unregistered persons).

📸 [Screenshot: GSTR9_Annual_RCM_Table.png] – Table 6 highlighting 6C/6D for RCM ITC declaration.

Audit Red Flags (How Taxman Catches You):

  • Mismatch in 3B vs 2B: Claimed RCM ITC that doesn't match supplier's RCM invoices in 2B.
  • Missing RCM on Freight in P&L: Officer multiplies "Freight & Carriage Inward" expense by 5% – if your RCM paid is less, notice issued.
  • Missing RCM on Director Sitting Fees: Ledger amount × 18% must match RCM declared.
  • Foreign Currency Remittances: Forex outward remittances (import of services) matched against IGST under RCM.

CHAPTER 11: WORKSHOP FAQs & COMMON PITFALLS

Q1: Forgot to pay RCM on invoice from 6 months ago – can I pay now and claim ITC?
A: Yes, pay tax + interest @18% p.a. for delay. You can claim ITC in current month's 3B (subject to time limit u/s 16(4) – Nov 30th of next FY). Interest paid is expense, no ITC on interest.
Q2: Hospital (exempt output) hired a GTA – do we pay RCM?
A: No. Exemption exists for GTA services provided to entities whose entire output is exempt.
Q3: Advocate not registered under GST – still pay RCM?
A: YES. Section 9(3) is absolute. Business entity must pay RCM and issue self-invoice.
Q4: Director full-time employee, monthly salary ₹2L, TDS u/s 192 – RCM applicable?
A: NO. Salary (employer-employee) is outside GST (Schedule III). RCM applies only to sitting fees/commissions (TDS u/s 194J).
Q5: Is there a threshold limit for RCM (₹5,000 per day)?
A: NO. The ₹5,000/day exemption was withdrawn in 2017. Even ₹100 RCM expense requires tax payment, self-invoice and GSTR-3B reporting.
⚠️ Critical Compliance Reminder: RCM mismatches are top trigger for GST notices. Always reconcile GSTR-2B, maintain self-invoices, and pay RCM only via cash ledger.
